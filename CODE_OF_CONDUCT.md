# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors, maintainers, and users must make participation respectful, welcoming, and free from harassment. Constructive disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, discriminatory language, intimidation, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not.

## Sensitive-data rule

This project handles email. Never post another person's messages, addresses, message IDs, Gmail URLs, attachments, exports, tokens, or identifying diagnostics without their explicit permission. Use synthetic fixtures in issues and pull requests.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately through the repository owner's published contact method. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports should be handled confidentially and without retaliation.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.

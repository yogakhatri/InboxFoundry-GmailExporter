6. Local file inspector Opt-in · local only

Select downloaded files to calculate real SHA-256 hashes, prove identical content, and search supported PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, image, and text-like files. PDF extraction supports bounded common stream encodings and embedded ToUnicode font maps. Optional English OCR runs locally from bundled Tesseract.js and PDF.js assets; no image, PDF, or recognized text is uploaded.

OCR is opt-in and slower. One session processes files sequentially: at most 20 selected images/PDF pages total, 20 MB per image, 50 MB per PDF, 16 megapixels per render, and 2 million recognized characters. Encrypted PDFs and SVG are not OCRed. Cancel terminates the OCR worker.

Redact detected patterns from optional text copies

Detection and redaction are best-effort suggestions. Review every copy before sharing; InboxFoundry can miss sensitive material or flag ordinary numbers.

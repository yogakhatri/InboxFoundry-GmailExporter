    Before your first export

    • Everything stays on this computer. InboxFoundry has no server, account, or analytics.
    • It reads your mail by opening each conversation in this Gmail tab, the way you would. Gmail marks each opened conversation as read.
    • Captured mail is kept in this browser until you delete it, so you can review and resume.
    • Keep the Gmail tab open while it works.
